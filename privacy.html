Hey Beepo Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Hey Beepo Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hey Beepo channel to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

What it accesses on TikTok, with the account owner's permission

  • Basic profile information of the owner's account (user.info.basic), to confirm it is connected to the right account.
  • Uploading the owner's own videos to their TikTok account (video.upload, video.publish).
  • The list of the owner's own videos (video.list), to confirm an upload arrived.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens TikTok issues are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other service or person.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Viewers

The tool does not collect any information about people who watch Hey Beepo videos. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to anyone using TikTok.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok's settings, which also stops all access.
