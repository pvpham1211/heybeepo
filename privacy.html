Hey Beepo Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Hey Beepo Publisher (registered with Google as "shorts-pipeline") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hey Beepo channel to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account and YouTube channel. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

What it accesses on TikTok, with the account owner's permission

  • Basic profile information of the owner's account (user.info.basic), to confirm it is connected to the right account.
  • Uploading the owner's own videos to their TikTok account (video.upload, video.publish).
  • The list of the owner's own videos (video.list), to confirm an upload arrived.

What it accesses on Google, with the account owner's permission

  • Google Drive (drive.file): uploads preview and cover images of each finished video to a folder in the owner's own Drive, so the owner can review them. It can only see files the tool itself created.
  • YouTube (youtube.upload): uploads the owner's approved videos to the owner's own channel, with title, description, thumbnail and a scheduled publish time.
  • YouTube (youtube.readonly): reads the status and public counts (views, likes, comment count) of the owner's own videos, and checks the owner's channel.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): reads the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, engagement and retention) for the owner's own statistics and reports.
  • YouTube (youtube.force-ssl): sets one of the owner's own scheduled videos to private when the owner rejects it, and reads comments on the owner's own videos. It never posts, edits or deletes comments.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens TikTok and Google issue are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never uploaded. Other results go into the owner's own Google Sheet and local files.

Data sent to an AI service

Two limited kinds of Google data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, only to produce the owner's own summaries:

  • The text of comments on the owner's own videos, without author names or IDs, to sort them into questions, praise, complaints and spam.
  • Aggregated channel numbers (counts and averages per topic, format and hour, with no viewer data), to write the monthly report's recommendations in plain English.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Sharing

Apart from the Claude API use described above, no data is shared with anyone. No data is sold or used for advertising. Viewer-level personal data is not collected.

Hey Beepo Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Viewers

The tool does not collect any information about people who watch Hey Beepo videos. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to anyone using TikTok.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops its access.
